Folio word list
================

Accepted guesses are five-letter words from ENABLE1, the Enhanced North
American Benchmark Lexicon compiled by Alan Beale and M. Bryce. ENABLE1 is
in the public domain.

Source used at build time:
https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/enable1.txt

Puzzle answers are a 2,500-word subset of those ENABLE1 words. The subset
keeps words that also appear in commonly observed English usage (filtered
with a public word-frequency ranking at build time) so solutions stay
familiar. The frequency ranking itself is not redistributed; the file
words.json contains only ENABLE1 headwords.

A short list of slurs and vulgar terms was removed from both the answer
list and the accepted-guess list so those words are not chosen as puzzles
and are not treated as valid guesses.

This list is not the New York Times Wordle word list. Folio is an unofficial
game and is not affiliated with The New York Times or Wordle.

Counts in this build: 2,500 answers, 8,616 accepted guesses.
