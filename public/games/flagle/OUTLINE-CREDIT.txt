Country outline paths in countries.json were simplified from mapsicon vector icons by Régis Freyd (https://github.com/djaiss/mapsicon).

The mapsicon README says: do what you want with them as long as you mention the author, and do not resell the icons. DLE HQ uses the simplified silhouettes inside a free browser game and credits Régis Freyd here and on the Bunting board.
